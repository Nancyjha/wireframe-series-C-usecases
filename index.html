LSPR Analysis
SMF Record Type 89 · Large System Performance Reference · hardware-defined LPAR MIPS ratings
📋
Version 1 — LPAR MIPS from SMF 89
V1 reads SMF 89 to extract IBM's hardware-defined MIPS ratings for each LPAR and compares them to observed peak MIPS from SMF 70. This tells you whether your observed utilisation is realistic relative to IBM's rated capacity. Full LSPR workload analysis (LSPR workload categories, consolidation modelling) is V2 — we'll contact you when it's available.
What MIPS capacity did IBM rate for each LPAR — and are we using it correctly?
LSPR LPAR Ratings
LSPR MIPS: IBM's Large System Performance Reference hardware rating for each LPAR configuration. Reflects the processor model's rated capacity at a specific workload mix.
Observed peak: Your actual peak MIPS from SMF 70 over the last 30 runs. If observed peak consistently exceeds LSPR MIPS, the LPAR's workload mix is more CPU-intensive than IBM's rating assumes — capacity planning may need to use a lower effective MIPS figure.
Note: LSPR MIPS values are model-dependent and change when engines are added or removed. The 10 Sep upgrade to PROD1 is reflected here.
